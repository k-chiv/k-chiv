Passion projectTinCan Translator™

Two phones, two languages, one conversation.

It started with a real problem: talking with a Portuguese-speaking classmate meant passing one phone back and forth in Google Translate. TinCan replaces that ritual — two phones pair once by QR code, then each person simply speaks their own language. A live transcript streams while you talk, and a clean translation lands — and speaks aloud — when you end your turn.

Sixteen languages run fully on-device; Khmer and Cantonese are powered by cloud AI and deliberately kept free, so no one's language is second-class. Designed, built, and shipped solo — idea to App Store submission in about 48 hours.

Idea → App Store in ~48 hrs 16 languages on-device No accounts · nothing stored Flutter · Supabase · on-device ML

v1 complete · App Store submission in progress · iOS first, Android planned
